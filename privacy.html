Privacy Policy

Money Manager Lite (Android package com.aifico.moneymanager.lite) by aifico · Effective 19 October 2026

In short: Money Manager Lite does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device, encrypted with a master password that only you know.

Information we collect

None. aifico does not operate any server for this app and receives no information from it: no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. The Android app requests no permissions, including no internet access, so it has no way to send data off your device.

Data stored on your device

  • Your financial records (transactions, accounts, budgets, categories, goals, schedules, shared expenses and imported SMS text) are kept in the app's private storage on your device.
  • They are encrypted with AES-GCM (256-bit) using a key derived from your master password (PBKDF2-SHA-256, 600,000 iterations). Your master password is never stored and cannot be recovered by aifico.
  • A few display preferences (for example navigation style or which ledger totals are shown) are stored unencrypted on the device. They contain no financial information.
  • App data is excluded from Google's automatic cloud backup.

SMS messages

The app does not read your SMS inbox and does not request SMS permissions. You can choose to paste bank SMS text, or select an SMS backup file you exported yourself, so the app can turn those messages into transactions. That text is processed on your device and stored only inside your encrypted vault.

Backups and files

When you choose Back up now, the app creates an encrypted backup file and asks you where to save it using Android's own file picker. You decide where that file goes (for example your phone storage or a cloud drive you use). Anyone with the file still needs your master password to read it. When you restore, the app reads only the file you select.

Sharing and third parties

We do not share data with anyone, because we never receive it. The app contains no third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising or tracking. Its bundled libraries (for charts and icons) run entirely on your device.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything from within the app (Backup & Settings → Erase all data), or uninstall the app, which removes its stored data from your device. Backup files you saved elsewhere remain where you put them until you delete them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
