In-app reference

aifico ledger User Guide

Version · Use this guide as a quick reference while working in the app.

Safety first: Before destructive actions, download and verify a full encrypted vault backup. Use dummy data to test unfamiliar workflows. Keep backup files private because they may contain sensitive financial information.

Your master password

First time on this device

  1. The screen says Create a master password.
  2. Type your own password (at least 5 characters). Avoid simple passwords such as a run of numbers.
  3. Tap Create Vault. Your data is encrypted on this device with that password.

Still using the old default password?

Vaults created by earlier versions may still use it, and the app shows a reminder after every unlock. Change it immediately: Backup & Settings → Security → Change password. Until you do, anyone who knows the default can open your data.

Forgot your password?

There is no "forgot password" — the data never leaves your device. You can recover only with a Full Erase backup plus its recovery code. Otherwise use New vault (this deletes the data on this device; a normal backup can be restored with its original password).

Good habits

  • Write your password down somewhere safe.
  • Back up regularly: Backup & Settings → Back up now.
  • New phone: back up on the old one, then Restore backup on the new one with the same password.

1. Start and protect your vault

  1. Create or unlock the vault with your master password.
  2. Keep the password private; do not share it with anyone.
  3. Lock the vault when you finish using the app.
  4. Use a strong password that you can safely retain. A recovery code is included only in a backup created as part of Full Erase; ordinary backups do not include one.

2. Dashboard

  • Net worth: what you own minus what you owe. It adds savings, checking, cash, investments and money others owe you.
  • Liquid cash: money you can spend now, in checking, savings and cash accounts only.
  • Income, Spending and Net are for this month so far, from Core Ledger entries, each with the change from last month. Transfers between your own accounts are not counted, and neither are excluded entries.
  • Tap a figure to see the accounts or entries behind it. Review figures against your bank if something looks unexpected.

3. Core Ledger

  • Use the ledger to review recorded income and expenses. It opens on the current month: use ‹ and › to move a month back or forward, or tap the month name to pick another month or All time.
  • Daily lists entries by day with each day's income and spending. Calendar shows the month as a grid; tap a day to see its entries. Monthly shows each month of the year with income, expenses and net; tap a month to open it. Total shows all-time figures, monthly averages and one row per year.
  • Each row shows the category in its colour, the payee and account, and the amount: green for income, red for spending. Tap a row for its details and Edit, Copy, Exclude or Delete.
  • Check the date, account, amount, category and description before saving or editing.
  • Imported records may need duplicate or reconciliation review; do not assume every imported match is correct.

4. Accounts & Wallets

Maintain the accounts used by transactions and review their balances. When a balance looks wrong, inspect related transactions and imported entries before making a manual correction.

5. Budgets, goals and schedules

Use Budgets to set spending limits, Goals to track financial targets, and Schedules for your monthly salary and regular payments. Keep dates and amounts current.

6. SMS import and reconciliation

  • Open Backup & Settings → SMS messages. Paste bank SMS messages, or use Upload Phone SMS Backup (XML) for an SMS Backup & Restore XML file.
  • Review extracted dates, amounts, direction, account and references before confirming or merging.
  • In Core SMS Ledger & Reconciliation, use Refresh matches to recalculate suggestions. Auto-reconcile applies only high-confidence matches; inspect ambiguous candidates manually before confirming.
  • Use the status filter to view pending, matched, merged, ignored, duplicate or error records. Merge to ledger is a separate action and may affect account balances.
  • Resolve ambiguous matches manually. A suggested match is not proof that two records represent the same transaction.
  • Use the SMS Review Inbox JSON backup for SMS review status; it is not a substitute for the full vault backup.

Planning a big purchase

  • Open Goals → Plan a big purchase and choose House, Vehicle, Land / plot, Education, Wedding or something else. Enter today's cost and how many years away it is.
  • The planner raises the cost by the yearly price rise you set (sensible defaults per type, e.g. 10% for education), grows what you've already saved at your assumed return, and works out the amount to save each month. You can raise that amount each year as income grows.
  • For a house, vehicle, land or education you can fund part of it with a loan: it shows the down payment to save, the loan amount, and the EMI after purchase as a share of your current income.
  • It compares the plan with your actual spending (average of your last 3 full months in Core Ledger): Comfortable, Tight or Short, and shows ways to close any gap: trimming flexible categories such as dining or shopping, a later date, a loan, or raising savings yearly.
  • Save as goal adds it to Goals with the target, date and monthly contribution. Other planned goals are taken into account in later plans.
  • All figures are estimates from your own assumptions. Prices and returns can differ; revisit the plan once or twice a year. This is not financial advice.

Balances, EMIs and duplicates

  • One rule updates balances everywhere: Income adds; Expense, EMI, Installment, Insurance and Investment subtract; a Transfer takes from the source and adds to the destination. Editing or deleting undoes exactly what was applied.
  • Every record has a hidden unique ID (UUID). It is included in backups, and an entry that appears twice with the same UUID is excluded from totals.
  • On phones and tablets, swipe left or right on any page to move to the next or previous one, in menu order: Dashboard, Core Ledger, Stats, Budgets, Accounts, Schedules, Goals, Shared, User Guide, Categories, Backup & Settings and SMS messages. Swiping is ignored while a form or dialog is open. Turn it off under Backup & Settings → Navigation.

Core Ledger header

  • The month bar, the views and the totals stay at the top while you scroll. Tap the magnifier to search (on wide screens the search box is always shown); the number inside it is how many transactions match.
  • Long ledgers show the latest 150 entries first; tap Show more at the bottom for older ones. Search, filters and the totals always cover every entry.
  • Tap Filters for type, year, dates, status and sort order. Choosing a year or dates shows every month that matches. On phones they fold away; filters that are on show as chips you can tap to remove. On wide screens they stay open.
  • Tap the settings button next to Filters to choose whether the header stays pinned, how filters are shown, and which totals appear (count, income, expenses, net, and transfers if you want them). This is saved on the device.

Stats

  • Stats shows where your money went in a month or a year. Choose Month or Year and use ‹ and › to move between periods; tap the period name to come back to now.
  • Switch between Expenses and Income. The chart and the list show each category's share, largest first. Excluded entries are not counted.
  • Tap a category to open its entries in Core Ledger for that period. Tap the category chip there to see everything again.

Getting around

  • On phones, the bottom bar opens Ledger, Stats, Dashboard and Accounts. Tap More for Schedules, Budgets, Goals, Shared & Split, Categories, Backup & Settings, SMS messages and this guide. On wide screens the same pages are in the side menu.
  • The + button at the top of each page adds the main item for that page (a transaction, account, budget, goal, event and so on).
  • Tap Lock in the menu when you finish. The app also locks itself after 5 minutes without use, including while it is in the background; change the time or turn it off in Backup & Settings → Security. You need the master password to open the vault again.

Add, edit and delete transactions

  1. Tap + and choose Income, Expense or Transfer at the top (More has EMI and Installment).
  2. Enter the date, amount, category, account and payee; subcategory and note are optional. For a Transfer, choose the From and To accounts.
  3. Repeat: choose every day, week, month, 3 months or year, and when it ends (never, on a date, or after a number of times). It is saved as a schedule in Schedules, and each entry is added to Core Ledger on its date, marked ↻.
  4. Installments (expenses only): split the amount into 2 to 36 monthly installments. Each one is named, for example, Laptop (3/12), and the last takes any rounding difference so they add up to the full amount. They are listed in Schedules with how many are paid.
  5. Save. The account balance updates straight away by the balance rule below.
  6. To change an entry, tap it in Core Ledger and choose Edit. The old balance effect is undone and the new one applied. Copy opens a new entry filled in from it, dated today.
  7. Delete removes the entry permanently and reverses its balance effect. If you only want it out of your totals, use Exclude instead.
  8. Entries dated in a locked (reconciled) month cannot be added, edited or deleted until that month is unlocked.

Exclude and restore ledger entries

  1. In Core Ledger, tap the eye icon at the end of a row to exclude it (the eye gets crossed out), or tap the row and choose Exclude. Tap the crossed-out eye to include it again.
  2. The entry stops counting toward dashboard totals, charts and budgets. It is kept, and account balances are not changed.
  3. To see excluded entries, open Filters and set the status to Excluded transactions. They are marked Excluded.
  4. Tap Restore and confirm to bring the entry back into your totals.
  5. Bank SMS: on the SMS page, Merge to Core Ledger opens the SMS as a transaction to check and edit. Save to Core Ledger parks it in the ledger as Excluded: it shows in the Daily list but doesn't count in totals or change any balance. When it's right, tap its crossed-out eye and confirm: it then counts, and its amount is applied to the account.
  6. SMS entries that match a transaction already in the ledger are excluded automatically as Duplicate, and their balance effect is removed. Review them under the SMS duplicates filter; restoring one puts its balance effect back.
  7. If you delete a transaction that SMS entries were marked as duplicates of, the app offers to restore those SMS entries so the money is still counted.

Select several entries at once

  • Tick the box on each entry. Once one is ticked, Select all picks every entry currently shown.
  • A bar appears with Edit, Change type and Delete for all selected entries. Cancel clears the selection.
  • Selection covers only the entries on screen, so filtered-out or excluded entries are never changed by accident. Narrow the list with search and filters first.

Accounts

  1. Open Accounts & Wallets and tap + (Add Account). Enter the name, type (Checking, Savings, Cash or Investment) and the current balance.
  2. Each account card shows its balance and how many Core Ledger entries use it; tap View entries to see them.
  3. The cards at the top show total assets and net worth.
  4. Choose the account new transactions use by default in Backup & Settings → Default account.

Budgets

  1. Open Budgets and tap + (Set Budget). Pick a category and enter a monthly limit.
  2. Each bar compares this month's spending in that category (counted Core Ledger entries only) with its limit.
  3. Total budget allocation adds up all limits so you can compare it with your take-home income.
  4. Use Edit or Delete on a category to change or remove its limit.

Goals

  1. Open Goals and tap + (Add Goal). Enter the title, target amount, deadline, how often you contribute and how much.
  2. Tap Link account to track the goal against an account: its balance becomes the amount saved.
  3. Each goal shows the amount still needed, progress and the next contribution date.
  4. For a house, vehicle, education or similar, use Plan a big purchase (see below) to work out the monthly amount first.

Scheduled payments and salary

  1. Open Schedules. Tap Set Monthly Salary and enter a title, the amount, the account it is paid into, the day of the month and the start date. The salary is added to Core Ledger automatically each month on that day.
  2. Tap Add Auto Debit / Transfer for a regular payment. Choose the paying account, then either another account (a transfer, such as a SIP) or a category (an expense, such as rent or insurance). Set the amount, frequency (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly), start date, and an optional end date or number of times. Choose the Income type for money that comes in.
  3. Entries saved with Repeat or Installments in the Add form are listed here too, with the next date and how many are done.
  4. On each due date the entry is posted to Core Ledger and the balances update. Each date is posted only once.
  5. Use Edit, Enable / Disable or Delete on a schedule to change it. A disabled schedule posts nothing.

Shared & Split expenses

  1. Open Shared & Split and add the people you share costs with under People. "Me" is always there.
  2. Tap + (New event) to create a trip, event or shared household and choose who takes part.
  3. Under Expenses, pick the event and tap + Add shared expense. Enter the amount, description, date and who paid, then how to split it: Equally among everyone, Selected people only, or exact amounts per person (these must add up to the total).
  4. Leave Record my share in Core Ledger ticked to add your part to your own spending.
  5. Each event shows everyone's balance: who owes and who gets back. Overview and Settlements suggest the fewest payments to settle up.
  6. When someone pays, use Record settlement. Once everyone is square the event shows Settled, and you can close it.

Categories

  • Open Categories and tap Add Category, or Add subcategory under a category.
  • Categories used by imports, projects, loans and schedules are created for you when needed.
  • Budgets and dashboard charts group spending by these categories, so keep names consistent.
  • Each category has its own colour, used in Core Ledger, Stats, Budgets and the dashboard chart. Tap Edit, then the coloured circle next to a category to change it.

What Lite includes

  • aifico ledger lite includes Dashboard, Core Ledger (daily, calendar, monthly and total views), Stats, up to 5 accounts, Budgets, Categories, Goals with the big-purchase planner, Schedules (monthly salary and auto debits or transfers), Shared & Split, SMS import and reconciliation, encrypted backup and restore, and dark mode. No limit on transactions or history.
  • Loans, fixed and recurring deposits, credit card statements, bank statement import and the aifico ledger spreadsheet are part of the Pro edition.
  • Both editions use the same data. A Lite backup restores into Pro with nothing lost. A Pro backup restored into Lite keeps all its data, but Lite does not show or post Pro-only items.

7. Backup and restore

Normal full encrypted backup

In Settings → Backup & Settings, use Download Full Encrypted Database Backup. This creates the normal encrypted backup and does not generate a recovery code. Store the file securely and remember its master password.

Restore a full vault

Select a valid full backup and enter its master password. For a backup made during Full Erase, you can use its recovery code if you have forgotten that password. Inspect the restore preview and counts before proceeding. Full restore replaces the active vault; it does not merge multiple vault backups.

Full Erase / Zero Reset is destructive. Its verified backup includes a separate recovery-code-encrypted copy. Save both downloaded files: the encrypted full-backup JSON and the separate recovery-code TXT file. Keep them separate. Before confirming erase, verify that both files exist and the code file contains the recovery code. The code is shown only for this reset backup; it is not included in normal backups. If the code is lost, it cannot be regenerated from the backup. First test with a separate browser profile and dummy data.

If an empty backup is detected while a populated vault exists, the app may require the explicit confirmation phrase RESTORE EMPTY. A corrupted or invalid backup should be rejected. After any restore test, verify account and transaction counts and inspect sample records.

8. Settings, categories and data cleanup

  • Use Categories to maintain categories and subcategories.
  • Use Backup & Settings to change the master password, run the data integrity check, manage the default account and access import/export tools.
  • Use Currency in Backup & Settings to choose the symbol shown before every amount (₹, Rs, $, €, £ and others, a custom code, or none). Only the display changes; your amounts stay the same, and the choice is saved in your backups.
  • Review proposed data-cleanup changes before applying them; unknown dates should be investigated rather than guessed.
  • Spreadsheet exports and SMS archives may contain private financial details. Protect them like bank statements.

Suggested routine

Record transactions regularly → review imported entries and duplicates → reconcile account balances → review budgets and goals → download a full encrypted backup periodically and before any reset or major restore.
